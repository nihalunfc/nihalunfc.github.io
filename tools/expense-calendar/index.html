Your data

Everything you enter stays in this browser on this device. It is never sent to anyone, including the maker of this tool. It stays until you press a reset button below or clear your browser's site data.

Backup

Reset

Each button clears only its own part. You get a few seconds to undo.

App and updates

How to install on your phone or computer
  • iPhone or iPad (Safari): tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
  • Android (Chrome): tap the menu, then Install app or Add to Home screen.
  • Windows, Mac, Linux (Chrome or Edge): click the install icon at the right of the address bar.
  • Mac (Safari): File, then Add to Dock.

After the first visit it works with no internet connection.

Lock with a passcode

Your expenses will be encrypted on this device with a key made from your passcode (AES-256). Nobody can read them without it, including the maker of this tool.

There is no way to recover a forgotten passcode. Keep a backup somewhere safe.
