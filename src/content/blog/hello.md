I'm Nihal. I studied physics first: a BSc at Government Victoria College and an MSc at CUSAT in India, majoring in astrophysics. In October 2026 I finished a Master's in Data Analytics at the University of Niagara Falls Canada. In between, I spent five years as a content writer turning science and technology into clear writing, and since moving to Canada I've worked in hotel housekeeping in Niagara Falls.

That mix is why I care about data that changes a decision. In astrophysics you model things you can never touch. On a hotel floor you see what happens when the schedule doesn't match the arrivals. Data analytics is where the two meet.

## What this blog covers

- **Building BI work end to end.** Warehouses, data-quality checks, dashboards and the pipelines that feed them, mostly with hotel data, because that's the industry I know from the inside.
- **Kaggle write-ups.** What I tried, what the leaderboard said, and what my own validation said when the two disagreed.
- **Mistakes and what they cost.** The first real post is about using a month of GitHub Actions minutes in one evening.

## How I write

Every number has a source. If a score came from someone else's public notebook, I say so. If my own idea didn't beat the baseline, I say that too. I think that's more useful to read, and it's the only kind of portfolio I'd want to hire from.
