A reproduction and out-of-sample test of von Foerster, Mora and Amiot (1960). Their equation tracked reality within about 11% until the mid-1990s, then broke: it predicts 96 billion people for 2025 against the real 8.22 billion. The article comes with the Python script, the data with sources and six figures in one [ZIP file](/downloads/science/doomsday-1960.zip).
