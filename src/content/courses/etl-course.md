1. **Ingest anything.** Landing zones, email, shared drives, SFTP and APIs, on a retail chain.
2. **Clean and trust the data.** Profiling, data contracts, Polars and DuckDB, Claude Code, quality gates, on a quick-service chain.
3. **Orchestrate with Dagster.** Assets, partitions, sensors and checks, on a hotel group.
4. **Load to the warehouse.** Azure SQL, PostgreSQL, DuckDB and ClickHouse, MERGE, watermarks and cost, on a marketing agency.
5. **Model it in SQL.** Star schemas, slowly changing dimensions, advanced SQL, dbt and a semantic layer, on restaurant brands.
6. **Deliver BI people trust.** Power BI and DAX, open-source BI, scheduled reports and reconciliation.
7. **Run it in production.** Observability, CI/CD, choosing tools, a low-cost open stack and a capstone.

All data in the course is synthetic, generated by a script you run yourself.
