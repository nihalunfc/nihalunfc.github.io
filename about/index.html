About

Nihal

I build things that turn operational data into decisions: dashboards and KPIs that managers actually use, forecasting and optimisation for planning, and AI systems that coordinate many small decisions at once.

How I work

  • Start from the decision. Who has to decide what, and when? The data and the model come second.
  • Use the data that already exists. Booking systems, meters and rosters usually hold the answer already.
  • Check every number. Results are tested on data that was kept aside, compared with today's practice, and reported honestly, including where something doesn't work yet.
  • Make it usable. Plain English, clear recommendations, and tools that work without training.

Areas of work

  • Business intelligence: KPI design, dashboards, reporting pipelines, SQL data warehousing.
  • Forecasting and optimisation: demand forecasting, revenue management, staff scheduling with integer programming.
  • Applied AI: multi-agent reinforcement learning, market-based coordination, learning from demonstrations.
  • Tools: Python, SQL, PyTorch, PuLP, JavaScript and D3, Excel.
