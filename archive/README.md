# Archive

Pages and files taken off nihalsulaiman.com but kept for later (nihal, 2026-11-22: never delete anything
permanently). Nothing in this folder is built or published: Astro only builds `src/` and `public/`.
Each item keeps its original path under `archive/`, so moving it back is a `git mv`.

- `src/pages/fields/`: the Fields pages (eight sectors). Taken off when the site was simplified; projects still carry their `field`.
- `src/pages/now.astro`: the Now page. The activity log (`/log/`) covers it.
- `src/content/lessons/big-data-cloud/` and `public/learn/etl-course/`: the old Data at Scale and Pipeline to Dashboard courses. Both were rebuilt on fibub (fibub.com/learn/courses/data-at-scale/ and /etl/); the Courses page links there.
- `src/content/tools/` and `public/tools/`: the business tools, rebuilt for any business on fibub (money shelf, small business section, #T1064 to #T1074; the expense calendar became part of fibub's bill calendar, the shift scheduler already lived there). The Operations and Workforce suites stay on the site (nihal, 2026-11-22). `public/tools/expense-calendar/` also stays: it is the "moved" page that lets people export bills saved in their browser on this site (and unlock passcode-locked data), which only works on this origin.
